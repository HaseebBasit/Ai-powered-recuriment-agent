How It Works

From Resume to Ranked Shortlist

Six simple steps, powered by AI, with the final decision always yours.

1
Post a Job

Recruiters add the title, description, required skills and minimum experience.

2
Apply with a Resume

Applicants browse open jobs and upload a PDF or DOCX resume.

3
AI Reads the Resume

The AI extracts skills, education, experience and projects.

4
Match Score

Each applicant gets a 0–100 score with strengths and missing skills explained.

5
Review and Decide

Recruiters see a ranked list and shortlist, reject or hold candidates.

6
Questions and Emails

Interview questions are prepared and applicants receive automatic status emails.
